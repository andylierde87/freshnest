Overview

PantryPulse is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not communicate with any server operated by us, and does not include advertising, analytics, or third‑party tracking software of any kind. This policy explains, plainly, what the small amount of data the app handles is used for and where it lives.

What PantryPulse stores, and where

Everything you enter — produce batches, quantities, storage locations, ripeness notes, scan history, shopping lists, and your app settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on‑device SwiftData framework. None of it is copied to iCloud by the app, uploaded to us, or shared with any third party. Uninstalling the app removes this data, and Settings → Delete All Data removes it at any time without uninstalling.

Camera and Photos

When you use the Scan tab, a photo you take or choose is analyzed on your device using Apple's Vision framework to suggest what the item is, and a local, deterministic image‑based estimate of its visible condition. The photo is never uploaded, and no image data is transmitted anywhere by the app.

Whether the original photo is kept afterward is controlled by Settings → Store Scan Photos. When this is on, the photo is saved locally alongside the scan so you can review it later. When it is off, only the resulting score and observations are kept — the photo itself is discarded immediately after analysis.

Notifications

Freshness reminders and daily summaries are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. They do not involve a push notification service or any server operated by us, and are only scheduled if you turn them on in Settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, email address, or sign‑in of any kind
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry sent to us
  • No data sold or shared with data brokers, advertisers, or other apps

Children's privacy

PantryPulse does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from its users at all — everything stays on the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Because the app has no account system, we cannot notify you individually — please check back here if you have questions about a new version.

Support

Questions, before or after you install.

Is PantryPulse a food‑safety app?

No. It gives a visual estimate of ripeness and condition to help you decide what to use first. Always check smell, texture, and the inside of the food yourself before eating.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. The food database, freshness model, and rescue recipes all ship inside the app. Core features work fully in airplane mode.

Do my scan photos ever leave my phone?

No. Every photo is analyzed on‑device. Nothing is uploaded, and you can turn off photo storage entirely in Settings while keeping the scan results.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Delete All Data, or simply uninstall the app — nothing is kept anywhere else.
